Recommend only, a human orders

The rules engine and Gemini never place an order. Every quantity is a suggestion until the planner clicks Accept or Override. Accepted quantities roll up into draft purchase orders by distributor.

Numbers from rules, words from AI

Actions and quantities come from the five documented rules and are covered by 16 unit tests. Gemini only explains them, and its reply is validated before it reaches the page. It cannot change a number.

Outside the rules goes to a person

SKUs with under 12 weeks of history and spike-versus-overstock conflicts are flagged for review. Every override is logged with a reason, which is how the undocumented 15-20% of judgment gets written down.
